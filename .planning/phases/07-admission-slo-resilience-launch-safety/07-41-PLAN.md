| T-07-41-02 | Tampering/Repudiation | provider mutations | high | mitigate | Persist exact operation before effect and reconcile only the original resource/operation after ambiguity. |
| T-07-41-03 | Denial of Service | Modal/Fly workers | high | mitigate | Bound resources, lifetimes, timeouts, concurrency, and cleanup; require zero backlog and finite refusal. |
